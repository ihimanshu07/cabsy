About CABSY

Built around a clearer journey

CABSY is designed to make cab booking requests easier to understand, submit, and manage.

A customer-first booking experience

CABSY brings trip details, vehicle choices, estimated fares, and booking records into one straightforward experience. Customers can create an account, submit a request, and revisit the information they shared.

Clear information at every step

Vehicle pricing is displayed as an estimated fare until a real-time operational pricing system is introduced. Booking requests remain pending until they are handled by an authorized CABSY process.

Designed to grow responsibly

The platform is structured to support future additions such as approved operational workflows, dynamic pricing, payments, maps, and real-time status updates without weakening account security.
